
# LunarSight Research Sources

## A. Coordinate and landing-site research

### 1. Wueller et al. (2026)

**Title:** Assessing Potential Landing Sites With Favorable Illumination and Accessible, Potentially Volatile-Rich Permanently Shadowed Regions Within Artemis Candidate Landing Regions.

**Journal:** Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets, Volume 131, Issue 3.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JE009434

**Used for:**
- Coordinates of HW10, MMA04, and NR101.
- Candidate landing site classification and terrain-screening criteria.
- Haworth, Mons Malapert, and Nobile Rim 1 regional descriptions.
- Modeled illumination and Sun/Earth visibility.
- Accessible permanently shadowed regions and temperature estimates.
- Limitations of the 20 m/pixel elevation model.

### 2. Kring et al. (2025)

**Title:** Notional Geological Traverses, Station Activities, and Sample Collection on Mons Malapert, Lunar South Polar Region.

**Journal:** Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2024JE008905

**Used for:**
- NASA's notional Malapert coordinate: latitude −85.964°, longitude 357.681°.
- Geological context of Mons Malapert.
- Detailed landing-slope and landing-accuracy assessment.
- Limitations of the available LOLA terrain model.
- Explanation of why the NASA notional point should not be treated as a confirmed feasible landing location.

## B. Official NASA sources

### 3. NASA — The Lunar South Pole Region
https://www.nasa.gov/reference/moonbase-environment/

Used for the scientific importance of permanently shadowed regions, water ice, and other frozen materials.

### 4. NASA — Shadows near the Moon's South Pole
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/shadows-near-the-moons-south-pole/

Used for the Moon's approximately 1.5-degree axial tilt, low solar elevation, long shadows, and permanently shadowed crater interiors.

https://science.nasa.gov/resource/shackleton-craters-illuminated-rim-shadowed-interior/

https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/scan/lunar-surface-propagation/

## C. Data and interpretation notes

- Coordinates in landing_sites.csv are reproduced from the coordinate table in Wueller et al. (2026).
- The selected points are representative research candidate locations, not final NASA-approved touchdown coordinates.
- The older NASA notional Malapert coordinate is a separate planning reference and must not be confused with MMA04.
- The 2026 Wueller et al. analysis uses a 20 m/pixel LOLA DEM for initial terrain screening.
- Small craters, boulders, and other hazards can remain unresolved at that resolution.
- Illumination results depend on the modeled period and are not guarantees for all dates.
- Ideal geometric Earth visibility is not equivalent to a validated radio link.
